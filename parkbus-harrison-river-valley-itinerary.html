One ticket covers the weekend's plans

Included in your Parkbus ticket

  • Return trip on a full-size coach from Vancouver or Surrey, with reclining seats, big windows, heating and a washroom
  • Family-style Saturday lunch at Sasquatch Crossing Eco Lodge
  • Time at the eagle viewing tower
  • Entry and programming at Kilby Historic Site
  • Lights by the Lake on Saturday night
  • The Sasquatch Museum on Sunday morning
  • A Parkbus ambassador with the group all weekend

Booked or bought separately

  • Your hotel room, booked directly with the hotel
  • The optional Sunday eagle cruise
  • Saturday dinner, Sunday breakfast and Sunday lunch

Good to pack: binoculars, warm layers, a camera and a swimsuit for the mineral pool.
